# Content Protect pilot readiness runbook

Status: preparation only — 8 September 2026. Do not invite or charge a creator until every applicable gate below has a named owner and recorded evidence.

## Purpose

The first pilot is to validate a limited, human-reviewed still-image discovery and evidence workflow. It is not a promise of complete internet coverage, automatic removal, video search, legal representation, or a platform/Google relationship.

## Entry gates

All of the following must be true before any real creator media is processed through a commercial provider:

1. TinEye commercial account, DPA/transfer review, and written approval references are recorded outside the repository.
2. A UK privacy review and copyright/takedown template review are approved by qualified counsel.
3. The production readiness endpoint shows discovery infrastructure and launch governance as ready.
4. The provider spend cap, usage alerts, and no-auto-top-up setting have been independently checked.
5. Yoti production age-verification and Stripe live billing are either approved for the cohort or expressly excluded from a free, non-commercial pilot.
6. At least five non-explicit, consented acceptance tests have passed against the approved source.
7. The operator on duty has completed the match-quality and evidence-capture process.

## First cohort

- Start with **five** invited creators; expand only after a weekly review.
- Only adults who are verified, control the submitted content, and understand the pilot scope.
- Begin with non-explicit reference images. Do not introduce video frames, facial recognition, or high-risk media during the first cohort.
- Do not ask for or accept creator-platform passwords.
- Do not promise removals, deindexing, response times, or source coverage beyond what the dashboard evidence supports.

## Invitation script

Use this wording, adjusted only for the individual creator:

> Content Protect is inviting a small group of creators to a limited private pilot. The pilot tests evidence-led image discovery and reviewed takedown preparation. It does not guarantee that copies will be found or removed, and no external notice is sent without your separate approval. We will explain what information is processed, which provider is used, and how you can delete your data before you submit any reference media.

## Creator intake checklist

1. Verify that the creator is 18+ through the approved route.
2. Confirm their stage name, contact email, and authority to act for the relevant rights holder.
3. Explain the provider, purpose, retention, international-transfer position, and deletion route in plain language.
4. Record per-file consent and rights declaration before upload.
5. Agree the included media type, scan frequency, query allowance, and support contact.
6. Explain that each match is a lead, not a legal conclusion.
7. Explain that notice preparation and delivery each require separate creator approvals.

## Operator checklist per scan

1. Confirm the creator account, plan/allowance, and per-file rights declaration.
2. Use only an enabled, approved discovery source.
3. Record source, timestamp, query asset reference, provider output, and any capture reference.
4. Review every surfaced match as `confirmed infringement`, `authorised use`, `false positive`, or `uncertain`.
5. Preserve evidence before preparing a case; do not label a notice as sent until delivery evidence is recorded.
6. Escalate disputed, unclear, urgent-safety, or cross-border cases to the named legal/support owner.

## Weekly pilot review

Record, per source and cohort:

| Metric | Minimum record |
| --- | --- |
| Usage | queries, spend, allowance consumed, remaining balance |
| Discovery | scans attempted, successful scans, leads, confirmed matches, false positives |
| Operations | review time, case drafts, notices approved, notices delivered, acknowledgements |
| Safety | consent withdrawals, deletion requests, disputes, incidents, unresolved risks |
| Experience | creator feedback, cancellation reasons, support response times |

Pause new invitations immediately if there is an uncontained security issue, a material privacy concern, repeated false-positive pattern, provider terms concern, or unresolved billing discrepancy.

## Exit criteria for expansion

The pilot may expand from 5 to 20 creators only after four weeks of documented operation, zero unresolved high-severity privacy/security incidents, an evidence-backed quality report, and written approval from the business owner and relevant counsel. Pricing and automatic billing remain off until Stripe live acceptance tests and cancellation/refund controls are approved.
