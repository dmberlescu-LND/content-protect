# Official reporting route register

Status: research register — 8 September 2026. Every route is **disabled for live delivery**. This register records public official entry points only; it does not prove a partnership, eligibility, removal rate, or authority to submit a claim for a creator.

## Operating boundary

- An operator may use a route only after a creator has approved the exact notice and the required rights, identity, source URL, page capture, jurisdiction and legal basis have been reviewed.
- Never automate a web form, bypass a platform control, create a platform account for a creator, or submit duplicate notices.
- A report to a search engine can reduce visibility without removing the hosting-page content. Treat host/platform removal and search deindexing as separate outcomes.
- Record confirmation/reference, recipient, timestamp, result and any counter-notice/dispute in the case timeline.

## Route-specific safety notes

### Google

Google differentiates product/policy reporting from legal reporting and may require a separate request for each relevant Google product. A decision affecting a Search result does not itself remove material from the host website. Start with the host or platform route when the objective is removal from the source.

### TikTok

TikTok states that false claims or misuse of its infringement webform may lead to suspension of use of the form or other legal consequences. A case must therefore remain blocked unless the creator’s rights and every reported URL are specific and reviewed.

### X

X states that a DMCA complaint may be forwarded to the affected user, including the complainant’s contact information. Use an authorised agent structure only after legal and privacy approval; do not surprise a creator with this disclosure risk.

### Reddit

Reddit distinguishes copyright/trademark reports from general rules and illegal-content reports. Choose the correct channel and preserve the platform confirmation; do not infer that a general report is a valid copyright notice.

## Before moving a route to approved

1. Confirm the official URL and terms again on the day of use.
2. Obtain specialist legal review of the route-specific notice content and claimant representation.
3. Document whether the route permits an agent, what claimant data is disclosed, and the counter-notice/dispute process.
4. Run a non-live or expressly consented acceptance test where permitted.
5. Add the route to the product source registry only after written operational approval.
